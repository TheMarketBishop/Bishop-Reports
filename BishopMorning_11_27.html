Learn One Thing · Why Gap-Ups Are Expensive

You could think of it like this

What you seeSPY up 1.6% before the open, everything green
What you payCalls already priced for that move, with a half day of time left

When a stock gaps up, the call options already have that move baked into their price before you can even buy them

The reason why that's important today is because it's a half day, so time decay hits harder, there's less time left for the trade to work

Theta is just time decay, it chips away at the contract as expiration gets close

The truth is, from what I've seen, chasing the first green candle on a gap-up is one of the most common ways people give money back

I cover how I think about this in my Why not to trade 0DTE session, hopefully that makes sense
