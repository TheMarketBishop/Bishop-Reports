00Glossary 🔗

Quick reference — key terms used in this report.

Flip LevelThe spot where dealer gamma flips positive to negative — 7,489. Spot sits 55 points above it and the gap is widening, so it's support, not resistance. Above the line the blanket absorbs shocks; below it dealers amplify.
Net GEXAggregate dealer gamma. Tripled from +$257M to +$844M in a single session — the largest one-day rebuild of the series — now 77% of the way back to the +$1.1B blanket that defined the May suppression regime.
Suppression RegimeA thick positive-gamma blanket where dealers sell rips and buy dips, compressing volatility. The May playbook that powered the grind higher — it's re-establishing itself as the blanket rebuilds.
Magnet CorridorThe band of call-heavy strikes that pull price. From spot to 7,700 every strike is a magnet ($719M across 7,550–7,700) with no resistance in between — the densest corridor the series has produced.
Weekly OPEXOptions expiration. Today is the 10th consecutive weekly drain — it strips near-dated gamma, but the magnets above sit on longer-dated expirations and survive. The blanket enters at its strongest pre-OPEX reading of the series.
Vol P/CVolume put/call ratio — at 1.28, the lowest reading ever recorded in the series (the oscillation ran 1.45–1.65). Institutions are buying calls at a pace that exceeds even the May rally.
Max PainThe strike where the most options expire worthless — 745 on SPY for today's expiry, a few points below spot. Into OPEX it's a mild downward tug; the gamma magnets above (7,550) pull the other way.
Memory Super-CycleRecord memory sales, HBM shortages through 2028, and unanimous institutional accumulation (MU/SNDK/Samsung/SK Hynix). The fundamental under the semis bid — and the reason the SK Hynix IPO matters.
SK Hynix IPOPriced at $149/ADS, ~$26B raised, reportedly 7× oversubscribed — trades today under the temporary ticker SKHYV before switching to SKHY Monday. Its debut strength is the read-through for the whole memory trade.
HBMHigh-bandwidth memory — the DRAM stacked onto AI accelerators. The supply-constrained heart of the memory trade and the reason the cycle is a super-cycle, not a normal one.
Hardware vs SoftwareThe rotation within tech: money flowing to AI infrastructure, not AI software. MU +232% YTD vs CRM −37%. Stifel's SHOP/TWLO upgrades show the Street differentiating within software, not abandoning it.
SeasonalityJuly has averaged +3.4% on the S&P over 10 years with a 100% hit rate, stronger in mid-term years. The calendar tailwind that has been running on schedule all month.

03GEX Structure 🔗

The blanket didn't just rebuild — it tripled. Net GEX surged from +$257M to +$844M in a single session, the largest one-day rebuild since the series began, and it's now 77% of the way back to the +$1.1B blanket that defined the May suppression regime. One more session like Thursday crosses the threshold.

The suppression regime is re-establishing. With the blanket this thick and positive, dealers flip back to suppression mode: they sell rips, buy dips, and compress volatility. It's the May playbook that powered the grind from 7,200 to 7,600 — dips get bought mechanically, and headlines get absorbed. The June-crisis mechanic (thin gamma amplifying moves) is behind us; the blanket is doing the work now.

The cushion is separating from spot — the signal. The flip sits at 7,489, 55 points below spot, and the gap widened from 36 points to 55 in a session. Every time in the series the flip has pulled steadily away from spot over multiple sessions, the market has made a sustained move higher. The regime boundary separating from spot is the signature pattern that precedes sustained legs up.

No resistance to 7,700 — every strike is a magnet. All ten top-volume strikes were call buying (a series first — ten strikes, ten calls, zero puts). Spot closed on the 7,550 max magnet, and the corridor from 7,550 to 7,700 carries $719M with no gap in between: 7,550 (+$181M), 7,600 (+$146M), 7,650 (+$98M), 7,700 (+$75M). The center of gravity at 7,608 confirms the pull is up.

Strongest Pre-OPEX Structure Of The Series
The blanket enters weekly OPEX at +$844M — the highest pre-OPEX reading the series has produced. The drain strips near-dated gamma, but the magnets above sit on longer-dated expirations and survive; the pattern from every OPEX since May is that the drain thins the blanket, the magnets hold, and the migration continues into the following week. The path of least resistance is up toward the 7,550–7,600 magnets — the risk is a failure back below the 7,489 flip.
04Greek Forces 🔗

Gamma — positive and suppressing. The rebuilt blanket flips dealers back to the stabilizing side: they lean against moves in both directions, pinning price toward the magnets and buying dips mechanically. This is the opposite of the June-crisis thinness — the structure now dampens volatility rather than amplifying it.

Charm — neutral into OPEX. With expiration today, near-dated time decay flows through the book, but it isn't a strong directional driver — the magnets and the pin dominate the tape. It clears the near-dated gamma without changing the bias.

Vanna — a tailwind as IV bleeds to series lows. ATM IV printed a fresh series low at 13.2% and the vol premium that peaked at 19.5% in the June crisis is fully unwound. Falling IV pulls dealers to buy back into the move — the grind-up fuel is running at full strength.

What This Means For Today
A fully constructive Greek backdrop — positive gamma pinning toward the magnets, vanna a tailwind as IV compresses, charm clearing near-dated gamma into OPEX. Lean the long side (memory/semis), keep careful entries on: the pin caps the upside near-term and the leadership is narrow, so size into strength toward the 7,550 magnet rather than chasing an extended breakout.
