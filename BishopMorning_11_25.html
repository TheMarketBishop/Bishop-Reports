DAY 3
$NVDA is a political story now
Trump said he's willing to pardon Jensen Huang "if necessary" and that the US taking a stake in Nvidia is "probably not too late". POLITICO says Congress is going after Nvidia, and there were more personal headlines on Huang from WSJ and Bloomberg. Why it matters: these headlines can hit any time of day, Nvidia is the biggest weight in the index so it moves everything
NEW
The bond market is getting nervous about AI spending
Oracle's credit default swaps (the cost to insure its debt) are now wider than Bear Stearns' were in January 2008. Lending to a hyperscaler for 30 years now pays 162bp over Treasuries. SoftBank fell over 10% in Asia. Why it matters: when lenders want more to fund the AI build, that pressure usually shows up in the stocks next
NEW
A string of big outages and a Treasury cyber breach
FinCEN, the Treasury's financial crimes agency, is investigating a breach and took some systems offline. Microsoft declared a "Code Red" after a 24-hour outage, and there was a ground stop at Chicago O'Hare. Palmer Luckey said the public will soon find out what's behind the outages. Why it matters: nothing confirmed yet, but if this turns into a cyberattack story it's a new risk headline in a thin holiday week
DAY 2
Tariffs: no new details overnight
Nothing new on the China, EU and 14-country tariff report since Tuesday morning. Why it matters: it's still out there, an actual announcement with numbers would be a fresh hit
NEW
China new home prices down 2.4% from a year ago
That's the 40th month in a row of declines. Why it matters: background weakness, not a mover today

Learn One Thing · Max Pain

You could think of max pain like this

It's the price where the most options expiring today would finish worthless, so the people who sold them keep the most premium

Today's max pain717 on SPY
Tuesday's close718.78, less than 2 points away

The reason why that's important is because on expiration days, price sometimes drifts toward it into the close, especially on a slow day

The truth is, it's only a tendency, a hot or cold PCE number at 8:30 can drag price far away from it and then it stops mattering

I'd use it as one more level on the chart
