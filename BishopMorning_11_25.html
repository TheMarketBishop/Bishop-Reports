Learn One Thing · The Gamma Magnet

You could think of the magnet like this

The magnetThe strike with the most gamma, today that's 700 on SPY
SPY premarketAround 703.77, less than 4 points above it

It's the strike where market makers have the most hedging to do, so price tends to get pulled toward it, and once it's there it can stick for a while

The reason why that's important today is because we're opening right on top of it with hot inflation and new tariffs just out

If it holds, you'll usually see price chop around 700 instead of trending, if it breaks, the hedging can flip and add to the move

I'd watch how price behaves at 700 in the first 30 minutes before deciding anything
