02Overnight Developments 🔗

The dip got bought, and the risk came off overnight. Institutions bought Wednesday's worst-internals day at scale, Trump signaled Iran wants a deal, and futures turned green and reclaimed the flip. The setup flipped from "brace for the pullback" to "the recovery leg is running."

NEWIran de-escalated. Trump: "Iran called, they want to make a deal so badly" — US futures turned green on the headline. He framed the strikes as retribution and added "it will get much worse if it happens again" (deterrent, not new escalation). The counter: Hormuz traffic dropped to 25/day (physical disruption finally showing), the Revolutionary Guards stayed defiant, and US strikes hit Iran's rail link to China and Russia. Net: the market is treating Iran as noise again — the third episode, faded a third time.

NEWInstitutions bought the dip — +$13.37B net, semis led. On the worst internals of the series, MU was the top buyer (+$4.39B), NVDA +$1.89B, AMD +$1.68B, AVGO +$1.23B zero sells (+$208M after hours), TSM +$350M. One seller: MSFT −$731M. The dark pool added +$2.54B — the largest of the series — with SPY +$868M (three sessions, $2.15B combined), QQQ +$832M, and even TLT +$232M (bought bonds). The crowd sold; the blocks loaded semis.

NEWRecord memory sales — $74.6B (+31.7% MoM). NAND +40.7% to $25.8B, DRAM $48B; UBS sees sharp price increases and supply shortages through mid-2028 (MU/Samsung/SK Hynix/SNDK). Hard-data validation of the super-cycle the institutions are buying. SK Hynix's ~$28B US IPO — reportedly 7× oversubscribed — begins trading tomorrow.

NEWSoftware is cracking as hardware rips. KeyBanc cut Salesforce to sector-weight on weak channel checks — a demand signal, not a valuation call. The divergence is the whole tape: MU +226% YTD vs CRM −34%. The money is flowing to AI infrastructure, not AI software — the rotation within tech that defines this leg.

NEWFOMC Minutes came in split. "A few" participants support hikes, but "many" see rates ending 2026 at or below current levels and "many" above — the committee is literally split. They flagged AI spending as an inflation driver. No clean hawkish signal; the payrolls-miss patience case survives.

CONTThe bull backdrop hardened. Financial conditions are the easiest since February (an 11-year high) on the equity rally and tighter spreads; the desk notes bears covered at length and July seasonality (10-yr avg +3.4%, 100% hit rate) kicking in. The IMF's paradox frames it: AI-hardware exporters are +4.4pp ahead of the world — the same force driving global growth and inflation.

03GEX Structure 🔗

The blanket is rebuilding and the flip is reclaimed. The +$431M thin structure that carried Wednesday's Iran shock held its floor, and with bears covering and IV falling on de-escalation, it's re-thickening — futures pushed back above the 7,468 flip overnight.

The flip flipped back green. Wednesday's structure entered thin (+$431M, a 36-point cushion) and held. Overnight, SPY reclaimed ~747 (SPX ~7,474), back above the 7,468 line. Above the flip the thin blanket absorbs and the mechanic turns constructive: the magnets above pull — 7,550 (+$105M), 7,600 (+$81M), 7,700 (+$61M) — and max pain at 746–750 reinforces the bid.

The thinness now cuts up. Here's the key: SPY-level gamma is still net-negative, so dealers amplify moves. That's what hurt on the Iran strike — but with bullish flow and de-escalation, the same negative gamma amplifies the recovery. This is the June-11 mechanic: when Trump called off the June strikes, GEX swung +$648M in one session and the recovery was violent. The structure that magnified the drop now magnifies the bounce.

The series says it resolves fast — and up. Every Iran episode this cycle (June 4, June 10–11, and now) sold off, then recovered within 1–3 sessions once the headline faded. The institutional +$13.37B buy and the +$2.54B dark-pool accumulation are the fuel the June recoveries had. This is the recovery leg, not a new leg down.
