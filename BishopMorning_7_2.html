00Glossary 🔗

Quick reference — key terms used in this report.

Net GEXAggregate dealer gamma. Positive = dealers sell rallies / buy dips, compressing ranges. Today +$478M — the blanket eased but held.
Vol P/CVolume put/call ratio — how hard institutions are loading puts. At 1.20 it's the lowest in the series; the hedging pressure that drove three weeks of chop is gone.
Flip LevelSpot where gamma flips positive to negative. Above it moves get absorbed. Today 7,413, 70 points below spot.
Magnet MigrationThe pattern where price is pulled up a ladder of high-gamma strikes. Re-engaged toward 7,550 then 7,600.
RotationLeadership shifting between groups without leaving the market. Today: out of semis/memory, into software, mega-caps, financials, healthcare.
Momentum FactorThe basket of highest-momentum names. Its realized volatility just spiked to levels seen only at major turning points — a consolidation signal.
Air PocketA sharp single-session drop in a strong name with no thesis change. In a positive-gamma regime these are buyable until the fundamentals say otherwise.
Leveraged-ETF LoopWhen leveraged and inverse ETFs mechanically amplify a move — the same feedback dynamic as a 0DTE gamma cascade. The engine behind Korea's selloff.
Dark PoolOff-exchange institutional prints. What gets bought vs the index reveals hedging vs distribution.
Dovish-At-The-MarginData soft enough to push the next Fed move later — here, a hike from October to December — without flipping the Fed to cuts.
Memory ComplexMicron, Sandisk, SK Hynix, Samsung — DRAM/HBM suppliers. Consolidating today, led by Korea, not by a demand change.
Midterm SeasonalityElection-year pattern: chop through Q3, rally Q4. July has been positive 100% of the time in this cycle.

02Overnight Developments 🔗

A firmer open the morning after a violent rotation. S&P futures sit +0.35%, the Nasdaq steadying +0.55% after yesterday's −2.57% tech flush, and VIX slips back below 18. The jobs print did the heavy lifting pre-market.

NEWJune jobs missed hard. Nonfarm payrolls rose just +57K vs the 110K consensus (private +49K), while unemployment unexpectedly fell to 4.2% on a slip in labor-force participation to 61.5%. May was revised down −43K. Average hourly earnings matched at +0.3% m/m, +3.5% y/y. The rate read: traders pushed the expected Fed hike from October to December — soft enough to delay, not soft enough to cut under a hawkish chair.

NEWThe dovish reaction ripped through commodities. Silver +3%, gold to $4,136, platinum and palladium bid, as rate-cut-adjacent trades caught a bid and the dollar softened. Metals are the cleanest tell that the market read the print as friendly.

NEWKorea's KOSPI halted −8%. A leveraged-ETF feedback loop — a 2x inverse product turning over 1,261% as SK Hynix and Samsung (together >50% of the index) fell — mechanically amplified the selling. The won hit its weakest since 2009, forcing dollar-driven foreign outflows. This is a concentration-and-leverage problem, the same gamma-cascade mechanic in a different instrument — plumbing, not a demand signal. It's the driver behind the memory pullback here.

NEWApple in talks for Chinese memory. Reports Apple is exploring purchases from CXMT and YMTC to diversify suppliers after last week's price hikes. A supply-chain diversification move, not a demand warning — and evidence the memory market is large enough to support multiple sources. Worth monitoring for MU/SK Hynix/Samsung positioning.

NEWOpenAI floats a 5% US-government stake (~$42.5B), framed as sharing AI's upside publicly and clearing political friction — with other US AI firms potentially doing the same. A notable escalation in the AI-and-government entanglement.

CONTRetail is leaving the Mag-7 — for semis. Retail was just 6% of Mag-7 volume last week, the lowest in four years, down 79% from the 2025 peak. The crowd rotated toward semiconductors right as the institutional tape rotated the other way — into software and mega-caps. Worth holding in mind on positioning.

CONTOil keeps sliding. WTI sub-$68 as Hormuz traffic recovers to ~40 crossings/day and Saudi exports surge (four supertankers, ~8M barrels), with OPEC+ likely adding supply Sunday. Tesla also flagged China sales +24.4% for an eighth straight month. The "ignore the noise" backdrop holds — geopolitics keeps fading, oil keeps bleeding.

03GEX Structure — The Blanket Held 🔗

Yesterday's question was whether the +$622M blanket could hold once the quarter-end bids and the JPM collar rolled off. It answered: net GEX eased to +$478M, but the regime held. The mechanical bids left; the structure stayed.

The stat that closes the chapter is vol P/C at 1.20 — the lowest reading in the entire series. The oscillation regime ran 1.35–1.65; the crisis peaks hit 1.69. At 1.20, institutions have stopped loading puts at the pace that drove every afternoon fade and every post-OPEX reload for three weeks. The hedging pressure that powered the chop is simply not in today's flow.

The call buying continued without the Q2 tailwind. Eight of the top ten volume strikes were calls, and the corridor from 7,500 to 7,600 absorbed +$175M of fresh call gamma. The magnet ladder above is the densest since early June — roughly $437M stacked from 7,500 to 7,600, with the 7,500 magnet pinning at +$120M. Institutions are building positions above spot, not hedging at it. The flip held at 7,413, IV held at 13.8%, and flip levels held at seven — all series lows or near it.

The Oscillation Chapter Is Closed
Three milestones confirmed in one session: the blanket held without mechanical bids, vol P/C dropped below the oscillation range, and call buying continued at recovery-session pace. The magnet-migration pattern that carried the tape from 7,200 to 7,600 in the spring is re-engaged — next targets 7,550, then 7,600. The suppression regime is back, and it enters July self-sustaining.
04Greek Forces 🔗

Gamma — suppressive. Dealers are long gamma into a +$478M blanket. The book thinned versus yesterday but still dampens moves; it's the mechanical source of today's tight 7,440–7,540 range and the reason the pre-holiday tape should stay quiet.

Charm — tailwind. With the quarter turned and a clean seven-level flip, charm bleed works with price rather than against it. The drift bias points up as long as spot holds the 7,413 flip.

Vanna — supportive. IV pinned at a record-low 13.8% keeps vanna a quiet tailwind. The vol P/C at 1.20 says the same thing from the flow side: the demand for downside protection that fed three weeks of two-way chop has dried up.

What This Means For Today
A thin, pre-holiday session under a self-sustaining blanket should be tight and quiet. The one caveat sits under the surface: momentum-factor volatility just spiked to levels seen only at major turning points. The index is calm; the leadership underneath is rotating violently. Trade the rotation, not the index.

09Sector Rotation — The Trade 🔗

This is the report. Wednesday's rotation didn't just show up on the chart — it printed in the institutional tape. The line the desk drew was blunt: hardware sold, everything else bought.

At the sector level: XLK −2.57% and industrials/utilities red, while XLC +2.44%, XLF +2.18%, and healthcare bounced (XLV +0.55%). Underneath, the block tape sold the four-name hardware complex −$8B (MU, INTC, AMD, TSM) while software (IGV +4.3%), mega-caps (META, MSFT, AMZN, AAPL), financials, and even duration (TLT) were bought. Microsoft's seventeen-week distribution broke. NVDA was the lone semi on the buy side — the market is distinguishing AI infrastructure from memory and legacy.

This Is The Move We Called
The rotation into software and mega-caps we flagged a while back is now live — confirmed by both prices and flow. Lean into it: software and Mag-7 as the early-July momentum lane, financials and healthcare as the supporting rotation. Memory isn't broken — it's consolidating on a Korea-driven air pocket, and it gets held through the stall, not chased. The froth coming out of the momentum names is the market resetting for the next leg, not ending the trade.

One positioning note worth holding: retail just rotated into semis at a four-year-high concentration right as institutions rotated out. When the crowd and the tape disagree this cleanly, the tape has usually been the better guide.
